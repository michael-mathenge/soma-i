# SOMA.i Demo Script

**Core:** 60 seconds. **Expanded version:** 3–5 minutes; use the expansion cue under each beat. The learner biography and drop-off problem are assumptions from `PRODUCT_BRIEF.md`, not verified research.

## 1. person

**Say:** “Meet Amina Demo. In this capstone, we assume she is a 23-year-old in Nakuru, works part-time in retail, and is aiming for a junior data role. She uses an entry-level Android phone with prepaid data, prefers English, and can switch to Kiswahili.”

**Fallback:** “Amina Demo is our seeded Data Analyst learner; the personal details are assumptions, not research.”

**Expand:** Point out that the app collects a minimal profile and supports English and Kiswahili.

## 2. pain

**Say:** “Our assumed problem is that Amina starts self-paced learning but drops off within 2–3 weeks: no one checks in, and it is hard to see how a reading connects to a job. We have no verified drop-off statistic.”

**Fallback:** “This is a problem hypothesis, not a measured claim; evidence is still needed.”

**Expand:** Explain how curated reading, checkpoints, and reminders answer different parts of that same assumed problem. Do not add an unsourced statistic.

## 3. on screen

**Say:** “Here is Amina’s seeded Data Analyst pathway in English. She can see her next checkpoint and curated low-data lessons, with source links and skill matches. Her weekly reminder is in English; it is also available in Kiswahili.”

**Fallback:** “If the live app is unavailable, the seeded pathway and lesson cards are shown in the prepared screenshots or described from the README flow.”

**Expand:** Open a lesson, show its publisher/source traceability, then return to the pathway and point out that switching, skipping, and restarting are supported.

## 4. wow

**Say:** “Amina submits her current checkpoint. On this result screen, the next skill is unlocked, low-data reading picks are ready, and matched opportunities show where the skill might lead. They are clearly labelled sample listings.”

**Fallback:** “If the result screen fails, use the saved screenshot and state that the opportunities are examples, not live vacancies.”

**Expand:** Keep Amina’s demo flow and reminder in English. Show Kiswahili as an available option for both the interface and reminder; it is not her default.

## 5. Codex

**Say:** “Codex helped us inspect the existing app, fix the result fallback, align content to the pathways, and add checks around the learner journey. We also made reminder operations reusable as a skill and scheduled task.”

**Fallback:** “If the Codex App cannot be shown, summarize the skill and Scheduled task and point to their documented acceptance checks.”

**Expand:** Show the `SOMA.i Reminder Ops` skill, the active `SOMA.i Reminder Dispatch` automation, and its completed **Run now** result. Note that the local automation requires the Codex App to stay open and the sender remains console-only.

## 6. next

**Say:** “Next we would validate Amina’s assumptions with real learners and evidence, then decide whether broader content or opportunity sources are worthwhile. Real SMS, accounts, live opportunity aggregation, and deployment are out of scope for this capstone.”

**Fallback:** “Our next step is learner validation; we will not present assumptions as proven impact.”

**Expand:** Invite questions about learner research, how to broaden curated pathway coverage, and what evidence would justify later work. Keep the stated out-of-scope items unchanged.
